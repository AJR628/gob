Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Every step is one recommended path with the exact values to enter. The fiddliest step — pointing your domain at your website — is two lines of copy-and-paste, and if you get stuck we'll do it for you.

Can't I just Google this? Or ask ChatGPT?

You can, and you'll get decent general advice. But it won't know your trade, it won't remember your business tomorrow, it won't know what you've already done, and it won't hand you your own hours and phone number ready to paste when Google asks. Launch does all of that. That's what you're paying for.

What if I already have a website, or a Google profile?

Tell us in the first 90 seconds and the plan adapts. Steps you've started become a quick check instead of a full setup.

Is this a subscription?

No. $79 once. Your account, your business record, and your export stay free forever.

How long does it take?

Honestly: a few weeks of occasional evenings. Some steps have real waits — Google verification can take days. The plan moves you to something else while you wait.

Does gobeefound guarantee I'll rank or get leads?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Launch gets your business properly established online. Customers come from being findable, reachable, and reviewed — which is exactly what the plan sets up.

What if I get stuck?

Every step has an "if something goes wrong" section. For the website itself, there's always the other door: we build it for $900, and what you paid for Launch comes off.
