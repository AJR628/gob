Questions

Will this get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What the site does is make you findable, readable by Google, and worth calling when someone lands on it. Combined with a proper Google Business Profile and reviews, that's what brings local customers.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, in your account. The site lives on hosting you control. If you ever want to walk away, everything comes with you.

What about after it's live?

You can edit hours, services, and photos yourself — I show you how. For bigger changes, get in touch; small updates are usually quick.
