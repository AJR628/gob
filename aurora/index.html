What I am talking about

When someone nearby searches for what you do, Google shows a block of three businesses on a map before any of the regular results. Most of the phone calls come from those three spots.

Being in there is not about having a website, and it is not about paying Google. It comes from a Google Business Profile, which is a free listing you claim and fill out. Most people starting a business have never heard of it, or they start one and leave half of it blank.

Straight talk about what this does

I cannot promise you will rank number one. Nobody honestly can, and anyone who does is guessing or lying. Google decides that, and it weighs how close you are to the person searching, how complete your listing is, and how many recent reviews you have.

What I can promise is that the listing gets set up properly and completely, that it is in your name, and that you are in the running instead of invisible. The reviews part is on you, and it matters more than anything I can do.
